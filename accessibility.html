Accessibility Statement

Unknown Worlds is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible.

It has been designed to be clear, simple, and usable across a range of devices and assistive technologies, using semantic HTML, with support for keyboard navigation and sufficient colour contrast for readability.

While we aim to meet WCAG 2.2 (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) (opens in a new tab) as published by the W3C (World Wide Web Consortium), some content may not yet fully conform. We are continually working to improve the site.

If you experience any accessibility issues with this website, please contact us and we will do our best to address them.
