Events

The site is hosting a small number of carefully managed outdoor events each year. These gatherings are organised by an experienced UK events team and are designed to operate responsibly within the surrounding rural landscape.

Events at the Site

The primary events hosted at the site are live roleplaying events. These are collaborative outdoor activities where participants take part in a shared fictional setting over the course of a weekend.

Participants camp on site and take part in organised activities and storytelling experiences in a structured and well managed environment.

Event Operations

Events are scheduled well in advance and follow a predictable annual pattern, taking place at limited points during the year, meaning the site remains quiet and undisturbed for most of the calendar.

Operational planning focuses on ensuring events run smoothly while minimising impact on the surrounding area. Traffic management, event scheduling, and on site organisation are all carefully coordinated.

Noise levels are also intentionally limited. There is no amplified music at events, and typical sound levels are comparable to conversation within a large gathering.

Event Participants

The events are supported by a large and well established community of participants from across the UK.

Many attendees have been involved in outdoor roleplaying for many years and contribute costumes, props, and creative ideas that help bring the shared experience to life. The atmosphere is collaborative rather than spectator based, with everyone attending taking part in the activity.

This sense of community has allowed the events to grow steadily over time and is a key reason for establishing a permanent venue where they can continue to develop in a stable and sustainable manner.
